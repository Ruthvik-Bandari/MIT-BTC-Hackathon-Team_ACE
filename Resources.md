| Resource | URL |
|---|---|
| Google blog post | https://research.google/blog/safeguarding-cryptocurrency-by-disclosing-quantum-vulnerabilities-responsibly/ |
| Full whitepaper PDF | https://quantumai.google/static/site-assets/downloads/cryptocurrency-whitepaper.pdf |
| CoinDesk analysis | https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/03/31/bitcoin-s-taproot-could-make-quantum-attacks-easier-than-expected-new-google-research-says |
| TheStreet summary | https://www.thestreet.com/crypto/markets/google-warns-quantum-attack-could-crack-bitcoin-in-9-minutes |

**Key data points from the whitepaper**:
- Shor's algorithm: < 1,200 logical qubits, < 90 million Toffoli gates
- Fast-clock CRQC: < 500,000 physical qubits, attack in minutes
- On-spend mempool attack: ~9 minutes to derive private key
- 6.9 million BTC with exposed public keys (1/3 of supply)
- 1.7 million BTC from Satoshi-era P2PK outputs
- Taproot increases exposure by revealing public keys by default
